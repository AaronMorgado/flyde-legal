Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Roamfly ("we", "our", "us") operates the Roamfly app for iPhone. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. Roamfly was previously called Flyde.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: your email address, first and last name, and home airport. If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple shares your name (first sign-in only) and an email address, which may be a private relay address.
  • Flight data: the flights you add (flight number, date, airports, airline) and anything you type into them, such as a booking confirmation code. We also receive flight status data (times, delays, gates, terminals, baggage belts, aircraft and position) from our flight data provider for the flights you track.
  • Trips and alerts: the trips you create and the alerts we send you about your flights.
  • Preferences: time format, notification settings and departure-reminder timing.
  • Device tokens: push notification and Live Activity tokens, used only to deliver your flight alerts. We do not collect advertising identifiers and we do not track you across apps or websites.
  • Subscription information: your plan and its status and expiry. Payments are handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or analytics about how you use the app.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To track your flights, organise trips and show them in the app, widgets and Live Activities
  • To send alerts about gate changes, delays, cancellations, boarding, departure reminders and landings
  • To send account emails (email confirmation and password reset)
  • To manage your subscription and the features it includes
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse (for example, rate limits on flight searches)

3. Service Providers

We use these providers to run Roamfly. Each processes data only to provide its service to us, under its own privacy policy. We do not sell your data.

  • Supabase: account sign-in, database and server functions (hosted in the US)
  • FlightAware AeroAPI: flight schedules and live flight status (receives flight numbers and dates, not your identity)
  • RevenueCat: subscription purchases and status
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, in-app purchases, push notifications and Live Activities, and maps (for premium map labels, an approximate position of the plane, not of you, is sent to Apple to name the region it is flying over)
  • Resend: delivers account emails (confirmation and password reset)

4. Data on Your Device

To work offline, Roamfly keeps a copy of your flights, trips, alerts and profile on your iPhone, shared with the Roamfly widgets. You can clear it in Profile › Clear offline data; it is also removed when you sign out or delete your account.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

  • We keep your account and flight data while your account exists.
  • You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile › Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and all associated data from our servers, and revokes Sign in with Apple access if you used it. Deleting the account does not cancel an Apple subscription; cancel it in your Apple Account settings.
  • Accounts with no flights and no subscription are deleted automatically if the email was never confirmed for a year, or if nobody has signed in for a year.

6. Your Rights

You can access and correct your information in the app, delete your account in the app, and ask us for a copy of your data or any other request by contacting us. Depending on where you live (for example the EU/EEA, UK or California), these rights are also guaranteed by law. We do not use your data for profiling or automated decision-making.

7. Data Security

We use encrypted connections (TLS), store your session in the iOS Keychain, restrict every database row to its owner (row-level security), and keep third-party API keys on our servers, never in the app.

8. Children's Privacy

Roamfly is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. We will tell you about significant changes in the app or by email, and the date above shows the latest version.

10. Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your data: support@roamfly.app.
