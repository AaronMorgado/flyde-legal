Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 16, 2026

Flyde ("we", "our", "us") operates the Flyde mobile application. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: Email address, first name, last name, and optional phone number when you create an account.

Flight Data: Flight numbers, dates, airports, airline codes, and confirmation codes you add to the app. We also receive flight status data (gates, delays, cancellations, times) from our flight data provider.

Preferences: Home airport, time format, notification settings, and theme preference.

Device Information: Push notification tokens for delivering flight alerts. We do not collect device identifiers for advertising.

Subscription Information: Subscription status and trial dates. Payment processing is handled entirely by Apple — we never see your payment details.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide flight tracking, trip organization, and real-time flight alerts
  • To send push notifications about gate changes, delays, cancellations, and departure reminders
  • To manage your subscription and trial status
  • To improve the app experience

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following services to operate Flyde:

  • Supabase: Authentication, database, and serverless functions (hosted in the US)
  • FlightAware AeroAPI: Real-time flight data (flight status, gates, times)
  • RevenueCat: Subscription management and receipt validation
  • Expo Push Notifications: Delivering push notifications to your device

Each service processes data in accordance with their own privacy policies. We do not sell your data to any third party.

4. Data Retention

Your account and flight data are retained as long as your account is active. Offline cached data on your device can be cleared at any time from Settings. If you delete your account, all associated data is removed from our servers.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data via your profile settings
  • Delete your account and all associated data by contacting us
  • Export your data by contacting us

For users in the EU/EEA (GDPR) and California (CCPA), these rights are guaranteed by law. We do not use your data for profiling or automated decision-making.

6. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (TLS), secure token storage (iOS Keychain), row-level security on our database, and server-side API key protection.

7. Children's Privacy

Flyde is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or by email.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or your data, contact us at support@flyde.app.
